How I work — guān xīn 關心, to care

The mechanism is never the point. These four principles sit under every engagement.
◆ 01

Start with the human

Deep user, clinical and field research before a single mechanism. I design for the moments that matter to real people.

◆ 02

Rigour is a form of care

Design control, evidence and safety aren't bureaucracy — in health, they're how you honour the people who'll depend on it.

◆ 03

Ship, don't theorize

Working prototypes over slideware. From cadaver lab to the App Store, I make the impossible tangible — fast, with tiny teams.

◆ 04

Technology for good

Design for humanistic solutions; technology to scale them and grant superpowers. A social-enterprise ethos, applied commercially.
